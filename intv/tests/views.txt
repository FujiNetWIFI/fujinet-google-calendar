# This guards the #evrec merge. st_day, st_week, st_agenda, st_event and
# alarm.bas each used to own a 16-bit pointer into SC_EVT; compose needed the
# slots, so all five now share one. That is safe only because no two of them
# are ever live at once, and this is what checks the claim -- a view that had
# its pointer clobbered draws blank rows or the wrong event's colour, not an
# error.
bp label_IN_POLL
hit
key 2
wait 40
snap
expect 0 WEEK OF
expect 2 DAY
key 3
wait 40
snap
expect 2 SU MO TU WE TH FR SA
key 4
wait 40
snap
expect 0 AGENDA FROM
expect 2 UPCOMING
# The agenda interleaves date separators into its display list, which is why
# vw_sel_event exists; a separator row is a date, not an event.
expect 3 SEP
